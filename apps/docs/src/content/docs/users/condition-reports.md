Condition reports record how a rented home was when you moved in, and when you moved out, for a
deposit dispute. Everyone in the location can read them and make the PDF, including viewers;
members and above record them.

## New report

A new report needs:

- The **location**.
- **Move-in** or **Move-out**.
- The day of the **walk-through**.

It starts with the location's rooms (places of the Room kind). Add any it missed, or remove one.

## Each room

For each room, record:

- **Condition**: Good, Fair, Poor or Damaged.
- A **note**.
- Up to **12 photos**. Each photo shows when Kept received it, in the location's time zone, and
  who added it. Photos lose their location data, as all photos in Kept do.

## Finish

**Finish** locks the report for good, with who finished it and when. Nothing in it can change
after, photos included.

- A report that isn't finished can be deleted by anyone who can change the location.
- A finished one only by owners and admins.

## Compare

**Compare with** puts the move-in beside the move-out, room by room (the same room by its place,
or by its name), the earlier one first.

## Make the PDF

**Make the PDF** produces the report, or both side by side when you compare, in English or Arabic,
with or without photos. A report that isn't finished says so in the PDF.

## Export and import

A Kept export of the location holds its reports and their photos. Imported, a finished report is
still finished and locked. See [import and export](/users/import-export/).
