Every shape the [location map](/users/location-map/) can draw, in the groups the shape picker
shows. Each has its picture, its size (width × depth × height, before it is turned) and the words
that find it in **Find a shape**, or that give a thing its shape from its name. This page is built
from Kept's own shape catalogue, so it lists exactly what your version of Kept has.

Sizes are typical ones: a thing keeps its shape's size on the map, whatever its real size.

**Missing a shape?** [Request it](https://github.com/keptinventory/kept/issues/new?template=shape-request.yml)
with a GitHub account: say what it is, give its size and add a photo or a link. Or
[add it yourself](/developers/add-a-shape/).
