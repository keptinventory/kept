Search finds things by name, brand, serial, ID, place, or the words AI added as aliases, and groups
results into things, places, people, shops and documents (with a snippet of the receipt text).

This page covers what you can search, filters, saved searches, offline search and semantic search.

## Filters

Filters narrow results by location, place, type, tag and state.

**Saved searches** keep a filter set for you or a whole location.

On a computer, ⌘K or Ctrl+K opens search from anywhere.

## Offline search

On a phone, Search also works offline from the phone's copy, labelled **On this phone · as of
last sync**. Results come from the local copy, and what you change syncs when you're back online.

## Semantic search

With an embeddings model connected, results "matched by meaning" join the keyword ones. For
example, a query about cutting paper could match scissors even when the word isn't in the query.
See [AI providers](/users/ai-providers/#search-and-embeddings).

## Scanning

**Scan** on Home and Search opens the camera. See [scan sessions](/users/scanning-sessions/) for
scanning several labels at once.

## Sharing a search

**Share this list** on Search makes a read-only share link for a search filtered to one location,
without state or price filters. See sharing a location.
