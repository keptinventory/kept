Lend a box with twelve things in it, and one doesn't come back, or comes back broken: each thing
keeps its own record of who had it, when, and what happened. Nothing is lost.

## Lend a box or a kit

**Lend** on a box works as on anything else: who it goes to, when it's due back, the condition it
goes out in, a note and photos. It is one **hand-over**, with a line for the box and one for every
thing inside it, all the way down, each remembering where it sat ("in Camera bag › Lens pouch").

- Something inside that is already lent, in repair or no longer in use stays here; Kept says so.
- A kit also lists its **parts not in the kit** ("Spare battery is in the living room"): tick the
  ones to lend too.
- Nothing moves: every thing reads **With Sam (in Camera bag) since 3 Oct**.
- **Undo** takes the whole hand-over back for 7 days, while nothing of it has changed.

Kept reminds you two days before it's due back, and when it's overdue. It never messages them.

## While it's out

Each thing can't be lent to anyone else. Moving it, trashing it or putting something new into the
lent box **asks first** ("This is with Sam: mark it returned first, or go ahead?"). Nothing changes
without you saying so.

## Return

**Mark everything returned** (on the box, or on the person's page) shows every thing still out,
each ticked **Came back**. For each, choose:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| Came back | It's back where it was; add the condition it came back in |
| Damaged | The same, with a note and **Update its condition** |
| Not returned | It stays with them; reminders go on |
| Lost | Its line closes as lost, and the thing is marked lost, never deleted |
| How many came back (a quantity) | The rest stays out on its own line |

The hand-over then says "11 of 12 came back". **Undo** puts every line back as it was. A thing not
returned is resolved later from its own page: **Mark returned**, or **Lost**.

## Where it shows

- **A thing's Loans tab**: every loan, with whom, what it went with, both notes and both
  conditions, and how it ended.
- **The person's page**: what they still have, grouped by hand-over ("3 things with Camera bag").
- **A box's Loans tab**: its hand-overs and how many came back.
- **History**: "Lent Camera bag to Sam", "Came back from Sam, broken", "Lost by Sam".

## Kits

A **kit** is a container with a parts list: what should be in it. Move a part out, lend it or lose
it, and the kit still knows.

- **Parts → Make this a kit** on a container: its contents are ticked as parts; add more by search.
  A thing is a part of at most one kit, in the kit's location.
- The **Parts** tab says **Complete**, "2 parts not in the kit" or **With Sam since 3 Oct**, and
  each part: In the kit, Elsewhere (with **Put back**), Missing, With someone, or In the Trash.
- **Check this kit** counts it with a [scan session](/users/scanning-sessions/): its list is the
  parts, wherever they are.
- **Edit parts** and **No longer a kit** can be undone for 7 days.
- Search and lists filter by **Kits** and **Incomplete kits**.

## Who can do what

| | Owner, admin, member | Viewer |
|---|---|---|
| See hand-overs, lines, a kit's parts | ✓ | ✓ |
| Lend, return, mark lost | ✓ (Lending on; returning works with it off) | — |
| Make a kit, edit parts, dissolve | ✓ | — |
| Check a kit | ✓ | A check: nothing saved |

Lending and returning need a connection. Offline, the status shows ("with Sam"), and a kit check
works as any count does. A Kept export carries hand-overs, their lines and kits with the location.
