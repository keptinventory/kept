title: Reports, insurance and claims
description: "Print an inventory PDF, make an insurance report with totals, record incidents and build a claim pack for an insurer."

Kept produces three reports from a location: a printable inventory, an insurance report, and a
claim pack for an incident. This page covers all three. For condition reports on a rented home,
see [everyday use](/users/everyday-use/#condition-reports-renting).

## Print inventory

**Print inventory** on a location makes a PDF of its things grouped by place, with photos and
short IDs, optionally QR codes, ended things and the Trash. Prices appear only for people who
can see money. Anyone who can see the location can make one.

- **Email it to me** also mails the PDF when it's ready.
- **Send it again** mails the whole location every Monday or on the 1st of the month; Settings →
  Me → Notifications lists these and stops them.
- A report over 10 MB isn't attached: print it from Kept instead.
- Both are locked, with the reason under them, when the server has no mail set up or your
  account has no email address.

## Insurance report

The **Insurance report** (where you can see money) lists every thing with its photo, brand,
model, serial, purchase date, price, current value and receipts, with totals per place and per
currency, as a PDF and a CSV.

**Also total in** adds one converted total when your exchange rates cover every currency.

## Incidents and claim packs

**Incidents** record a burglary, fire, flood or loss: select things on a location's list, then
add them to an incident.

A **claim pack** is a ZIP of the insurance report with every receipt, invoice, photo and serial
for the incident, shared with the insurer by a download link that lasts 1 to 7 days, is shown
once and can be revoked. Owners and admins make them.
