## What to try

- Tap a room, a shelf or a box: the card says where it is and what's inside. **Details** has the rest: its type and tags, a photo on some things, and its label's QR code.
- **What's in** under the map lists every room's things. Find one by name or by the short ID on its label, pick a floor, or switch to **Needs attention**. A code shows as amber tape only on things with a printed label.
- **Plan** looks straight down. The floor buttons (**G**, **F**, **R**) go up and down; **All** stacks them.
- **Low stock**, **Lent out** and **Due soon** turn their pins on and off.
- **Arrange**: drag things, hold a room's floor and drag it somewhere else (what's in it goes too), resize a room by its amber corner, draw a room. **Put on…** puts a small thing on a desk or shelf. **Done** keeps it until you leave the page, and offers **Undo**; **Cancel** asks first.
- Turn off **Detailed models** and the map downloads no models at all.
