title: Lending and borrowing
description: "Who has what, and when it's due back. Lend to anyone by name, no account needed; reminders before and after the due date; one list of everything lent out and borrowed in."
---

Kept remembers who has your things. Lend the drill to a neighbour with a due date, and Kept
reminds you two days before it's due and again when it's late. Things you borrowed are tracked the
same way.

## Turn it on

Lending is a module: **Location settings → What to track → Lending**. It's part of the
**Household** and **Complete** presets. A new account's Personal location starts on
**Essentials**, so turn it on there if you lend from Personal
([modules and presets](/users/modules/)).

Where it's off, **Lend** on a thing and the **Lending** page say so. An owner or admin of the
location gets **Turn on Lending here**: one tap, and the lend form opens. Anyone else sees who can
turn it on.

## Lend something

Open the thing and choose **Lend**:

- **To whom**: a member of the location, one of your contacts, or **+ New person** for anyone
  else. A borrower is just a name: no account, no email, no sign-in.
- **How many**, for a thing with a quantity: the part you lend gets its own line and joins the
  rest again when it comes back.
- **Due date** (optional), a note, the condition it goes out in, and photos of it.

A box goes with everything inside it, each thing on its own line, so each one comes back (or
doesn't) on its own record. A kit lists its parts kept elsewhere too. See
[kits and hand-overs](/users/kits/).

## Borrowed things

Record what you borrowed from someone, with where it is and when it goes back. It shows on the
same lists, under **In: borrowed**; what you lent is under **Out: lent**.

## What's out, and with whom

- **Lending**, in the menu: everything lent out and borrowed in, across your locations, overdue
  first. Filter by person, location or state (out now, overdue, returned), and group by person or
  location.
- **Home**: the **Lent out** tile counts what's out; tap it for the list.
- **A person's page**: what they have of yours, what they lent you, and the history.

## Reminders

Two days before the due date, and when a loan is overdue, by every channel you've turned on: the
app, mail, push, ntfy, Telegram ([reminders and notifications](/users/reminders/)). Kept never
contacts the borrower. **Copy a reminder** gives you a short, friendly message, in your language,
to send them yourself.

## When it comes back

**Mark returned**: back to where it was, or somewhere else, with the condition it came back in
and a note.

- **Damaged**, under **What happened**, if it came back damaged. The condition it came back in is
  **Poor** unless you choose another, and the thing's own condition takes it. Switch off **Update
  its condition** to leave the thing's condition as it was.
- **Taken back by**: you, or another member of the location who took it back. For something you
  borrowed, it's **Given back by**.

A hand-over is returned line by line: came back, damaged, not returned, or lost, and one member
took it all back. A lost thing is marked lost, never deleted. Every step can be undone.

The assistant and MCP clients return a thing with the same choices: damaged, a note, and who took
it back.

## Who had it

On a thing's **Loans** tab, **Who had it** lists every loan of it, newest first: who had it, from
when to when, how it came back (returned, returned late, came back damaged, lost, the condition it
came back in) and who took it back. A loan stays when it comes back, so the list goes back to the
thing's first loan. Viewers see it too, without the buttons. Returns recorded before Kept 1.14.7
don't say who took them back.

## Letting family or friends look

A borrower doesn't need an account. When someone should see the inventory themselves:

- **Invite them as a viewer**, with an end date if it's for a while
  ([sharing a location](/users/sharing/)).
- **Send a share link**: read-only, no account, for a box, a place or a search.
