---
title: The graph
description: See a location as a graph of its floors, rooms, boxes and things, in 2D or 3D, with what needs attention and your tags.
---

A location's page has five layouts: **List**, **Grid**, **Graph**, **Table** and **Map**. Table
is on wide screens only, so a phone shows List · Grid · Graph · Map. **Graph** draws the location
as nodes and links: the location, its floors, the rooms on them, the shelves, cupboards and boxes
in the rooms, and the things in those. Kept remembers your choice for each location on each
device. A floor's or a room's page has **Graph** too, opened on that place.

The graph needs a connection, like the map: it isn't part of the phone's offline copy. It loads
only when you open it.

## 2D

The 2D graph shows one place in the middle and two levels around it: what is directly in it on
the first ring, and what is in those on the second, when there is room for it.

- **Size** shows how much is inside: a bigger node holds more things.
- A node whose contents aren't drawn shows their **count**.
- **Pick** a node to see its card. **Pick it again**, or **Go in** on the card, to put it in the
  middle.
- The **path** above the graph (Home › Ground floor › Garage) goes back up.
- **Rooms · Boxes · All** chooses how deep it draws: places only, places and boxes, or
  everything down to the things.

Drag to move the graph, scroll or pinch to zoom, and the house button shows the whole location
again.

## 3D

**3D** shows the whole location at once: the location above, each floor as a layer at its own
height, the rooms on a ring around their floor, and what they hold fanned out beyond them. A
location with no floors is one layer.

It turns slowly by itself until you touch it, and starts still when your device asks for less
motion. Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom. The buttons beside it turn it left and right,
look from higher or lower, zoom, start or stop the turning, and show the whole house. The floor
buttons (as on the map) bring one floor forward; **All** shows every floor.

Picking a node brings its branch forward: everything outside it fades, and it names what is in
it. Kept remembers 2D or 3D for you on each device.

## Pins and tags

Dots on a node show what needs attention inside it, in the map's colours: **Low stock** (red),
**Lent out** (blue) and **Due soon** (orange). They come from the same places as the map's pins:
Consumables, Lending, and schedules, warranties and documents. Each node's name for a screen
reader says them too ("Garage, Room, 9 things, 1 lent out").

Turn on **Tags** to add your tags as chips in their own colours, each linked to the places and
boxes that hold the things it is on. Pick a tag to list what carries it.

## The card

The card shows where a node is, what it is, how many things are inside, its pins and what is in
it (pick any of them). **Open** goes to its page, **Show on the map** opens the map on it (or on
the place that holds a thing), and **Go in** puts it in the middle of the 2D graph. On a phone the
card is a sheet at the bottom; **×** or Escape closes it.

## With a keyboard or a screen reader

Every node is a button. Tab moves through them in the tree's order, Enter picks one and Enter
again goes in, and Escape closes the card and then goes up a level. Below the graph, a list of
what it shows (for screen readers, and shown when you tab into it) does the same.

## Large locations

Kept draws up to 2,000 things in a graph. Past that, the rest are counted in their places and
boxes but not drawn. In 3D, a location with more than about 450 places, boxes and things shows
the things only for the branch you picked.
