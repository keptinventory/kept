---
title: Sharing a location
description: "Invite people with a role and an end date, share links for read-only access, and print a list on paper."
---

A location is shared by inviting people to it, each with a role and an optional end date. Your
Personal location is never shared: it has no members, invites or share links.

This page covers invites, roles, share links and printing.

## Inviting people

The **Invite people to …** card on Home appears after you create a location. The same page is under
**Location settings → Members → Invite**.

1. Choose the **Role**, and under **Access** either **No end date** or **Until a date**. Their
   access stops on that date.
2. **Create invite link.** The link works once, for 7 days. Show the QR code to scan with their
   phone, or copy the link. A pending invite shows under **Members** until it's used, and you can
   revoke it there.
3. **Send by email** works only when the server has email set up.

Anyone but the owner can **Leave this location**. An instance admin can't see inside a home unless
they've been invited to it.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do in that location |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including two-factor rules, transfer and deletion. There is one owner. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members and viewers, change settings and modules, import and export, make share links and claim packs, delete permanently. Only the owner grants or removes admins. |
| **Member** | Add, edit, move, lend and trash things; log readings and services; print labels; use AI capture. Money is visible. |
| **Viewer** | Look and search only. Money is hidden unless the location allows viewers to see it. The assistant answers but can't change anything. |

## Share links

Share links show someone without an account what's in a box, a place or a search, read-only.
Owners and admins make one from:

- A box's **Actions → Share a read-only link**.
- A place's **Manage** section.
- **Share this list** on Search: a search filtered to one location, without state or price filters.

Choose how long it works: a day, a week, 30 days (the default), 90 days or a year. The link is
shown once, so copy it then.

### What they see

Each thing's name, photo, type, where it is, quantity, condition and notes, A to Z. Never prices,
serial numbers, secret fields, people, loans or documents. Things in the Trash, drafts and things
you sold or lost don't show.

### It stays current

The page shows the box, place or search as it is when it's opened. A search's link matches its
words and its place, type and tag filters.

### Managing links

**Location settings → Share links** lists every link with its state and how often it was opened.

- **Preview as recipient** shows the page as the recipient sees it.
- **Revoke** ends it at once.
- A link also stops when it expires, or when the person who made it no longer runs the location.

## Printing a list

To hand someone a list on paper, print it. **Print inventory** on a location makes a PDF of its
things, grouped by place, with photos and short IDs. Prices appear only for people who can see
money. Anyone who can see the location can make one.

**Email it to me** also mails the PDF when it's ready, and **Send it again** mails the whole
location every Monday or on the 1st of the month. Settings → Me → Notifications lists these and
stops them. A report over 10 MB isn't attached: print it from Kept instead.
