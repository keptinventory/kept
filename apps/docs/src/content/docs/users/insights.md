title: Insights

---

**Insights** (in the sidebar, and under More on a phone) shows figures across your locations with
Money on, or one location picked with the **Location** chip.

## Spending on things

Confirmed purchases, grouped by the year you bought them.

- A purchase counts its total, or its priced lines when it has no total.
- A purchase with neither is listed as having no amount.
- This year is shown on its own, "so far", never as a bar beside full years.

## Value by place

Each thing in use, at its latest valuation, per top-level place. A thing in a box in a cupboard
counts in the room.

Things without a valuation are counted but add nothing: Kept never guesses a value.

## Vehicle costs

Where Vehicles is on: fuel, service and fees across your vehicles for the last 12 full months, and
each vehicle's last two years and this year so far. These are the same figures as a vehicle's
Costs tab. See vehicles and machines.

## Warranty coverage

Where Warranties & claims is on: how many things are under warranty, ending soon, past their cover
or without one, what ends soon, and the repairs resolved this year and last.

## Currencies and hidden amounts

- Amounts are shown per currency and are never added across currencies or converted.
- Where money is hidden from you, Insights shows counts and says which locations' amounts it leaves
  out.
