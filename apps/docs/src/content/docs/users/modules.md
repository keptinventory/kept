title: Modules and presets

Every location runs on a **preset** — a set of **modules** switched on together. A preset is a
starting point, not a cage: an owner or an admin can switch single modules on or off afterwards,
and turning one off only hides it. Nothing is ever deleted.

You choose the preset when the location is created ([Your first minutes](/users/first-run/)).
This page covers what each one brings, and what the switches do.

## The three presets

- **Essentials** — finding things: rooms, boxes, labels and search.
- **Household** — adds receipts and warranties, reminders, lending, paperwork and vehicles.
- **Complete** — adds fuel, things you run out of, passwords and codes, moving house, and
  "Connect ChatGPT or Claude".

The timezone and currency come from your browser when the location is made; both can be changed
later in **Location settings**, next to the preset.

## What each module adds

The switch names below are the ones you see in **What to track**.

| Module | First in | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| **Labels and QR codes** | Essentials | Printable labels for things and places; scanning a label opens the thing. |
| **Money and spending** | Household | Prices, totals and **Insights** (Insights is part of Money). Each currency on its own line, never converted. |
| **Receipts and warranties** | Household | Warranty dates that expire; an expiring warranty shows as a reminder. |
| **Reminders** | Household | Schedules: when something is due, and what runs out. |
| **Lending** | Household | Lending things out and borrowing them in. |
| **Paperwork** | Household | The paperwork library; document dates feed reminders. |
| **Vehicles** | Household | Vehicles and machines. |
| **Fuel** | Complete | Fuel and charging. Needs **Vehicles**. |
| **Things you run out of** | Complete | A minimum stock ("keep at least 4"); low stock shows as a reminder. |
| **Moving house** | Complete | Moving mode. Needs **Labels and QR codes**. |
| **Passwords and codes** | Complete | Secret fields: hidden until someone allowed to reveals one, which is logged. |
| **Connect ChatGPT or Claude** | Complete | The Kept MCP connection, so Claude or ChatGPT can work with your location. |

**AI capture** and the **AI assistant** are in every preset and come on once an AI provider is
connected for the location. Both have their own switch, so a provider can be connected while the
AI features stay off in a particular location.

## Turning a module off

A module that is off is hidden everywhere it would appear — its screens, its entries in the
navigation, its filters — and its reminders pause. Switching it back on brings everything back,
because nothing was deleted.

Three details worth knowing:

- **Reminders pause with their module.** Schedules and thing expiries wait with **Reminders**,
  warranty and registration dates with **Receipts and warranties**, document dates with
  **Paperwork**, loans with **Lending**, low stock with **Things you run out of**. Stale-reading
  reminders are core: they keep running whatever is switched off.
- **Dependents go too.** Fuel needs Vehicles, and Moving needs Labels: switching the one a module
  depends on also switches the module off. The list shows it as *Needs Vehicles*, and the page
  lists what will turn off before you save.
- **Navigation follows your locations.** A module's entry (Schedules, Lending, Paperwork,
  Vehicles, Things you run out of, Moving, Insights) shows once the module is on in any of your
  locations. Notifications, Activity and Trash are core and are always there.

## Changing it later

An owner or an admin opens the location's **Location settings** and **What to track**. The
**Preset** picker sets every module at once; below it, one switch each (**Fine-tune**) for the
rest. Before saving, the page lists what will turn on and what will turn off, so a change is
never a surprise.
