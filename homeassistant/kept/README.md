# Kept

A home inventory you host yourself: where things are, what they are, and what's due (warranties,
services, loans, low stock). Runs with its own database; nothing leaves your Home Assistant.

- Phones scan labels and add photos.
- The Kept integration brings what's due into Home Assistant.
- Backed up with your Home Assistant backups.

Experimental while Home Assistant's own testing of the app finishes.
